I — The Temple

Step inside, at your own pace.

Myohokkein is a Nichiren Buddhist temple that has stood in the heart of Kobe for over 140 years.
Unlike traditional wooden temples in Japan, it is a modern, building-like temple inspired by the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Dome.

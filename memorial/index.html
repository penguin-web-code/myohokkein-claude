Animal memorial

動物供養碑　—　Animal Memorial Garden

Animal memorial and ossuary services are available.

At Myohokkein, pets are memorialised in a shared ossuary beneath a monument engraved with a phoenix.
The ashes of pets that have already been cremated are interred together there.

More than 1,000 animals rest beneath the monument — including dogs, cats, hamsters, birds, lizards, turtles, pigs, and many others.

At the time of interment, a Buddhist memorial rite is performed together with the temple priests, including the chanting of sutras. The ceremony usually lasts around 20–30 minutes.

The animal memorial monument may be visited at any time, day or night. Please be assured that the site is cared for daily, with priests offering prayers and maintaining the grounds each morning.

The Animal Memorial Garden is open 24 hours a day for visits.

How to Apply for
Animal Memorial Services

Please message us on Instagram with your name, your pet’s name, your pet’s type (dog, cat, etc.), and a note that you would like to arrange interment.

We will reply in English to discuss the date and details with you.

Interment costs ¥33,000, plus an additional ¥7,000–¥20,000 depending on the size of the urn.
